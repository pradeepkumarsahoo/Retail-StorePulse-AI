Problem this solves

Energy waste: the building runs on a timetable, not on the store. HVAC is usually the largest energy load in a non-food store, and its schedule was set when the store opened. It starts at 05:00, the floor is warm by 06:15, and the doors open at 09:00. It runs until 22:00, an hour after close, and heats the dock for a 07:00 truck the TMS already shows has slipped. StorePulse already forecasts when customers and trucks arrive, so this screen finds the hours the store is conditioned while empty and prices them before the next bill. Nothing is written to the building system, and comfort at opening is a hard limit.
