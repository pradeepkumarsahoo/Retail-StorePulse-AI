Problem this solves

TMS disconnected from labor planning. The TMS knows on Monday that Thursday's truck will slip to Friday; the store finds out Thursday at 7am, after the receiving crew has clocked in. This screen pulls the 5-day truck outlook into the plan so idle hours are predicted and re-sequenced before the day starts — no employee is named, no schedule is auto-changed.
